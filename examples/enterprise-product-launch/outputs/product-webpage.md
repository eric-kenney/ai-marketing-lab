## Problem

### Headline
Teams waste hours searching and still question the answer

### Copy
Company knowledge lives in many systems. Traditional search returns files, not answers, forcing employees to open documents and piece together information. Public AI assistants don’t have access to internal content and often can’t point to authoritative sources. The result: fragmented knowledge, repetitive questions, slow onboarding, and uncertainty about whether information is current or accurate.

## Value Proposition

### Headline
A single place to ask—and trust—the right answer

### Copy
With Atlas Knowledge, employees ask a question in natural language and receive a concise response backed by citations to approved company content. Instead of navigating tools and guessing which document is right, they verify the source and move forward. Work speeds up, interruptions drop, and new hires learn faster.

## Key Benefits

### Trusted answers employees can act on
**Benefit copy**  
Employees gain confidence to make decisions because every response is grounded in approved company information and linked to the original source.

**How it works**  
Atlas Knowledge uses retrieval-augmented generation to generate answers from your internal content and includes citations to the specific documents or records it used.

### One place to access company knowledge
**Benefit copy**  
People find what they need without knowing which system holds it—no content migration or new workflows required.

**How it works**  
The platform connects to existing document repositories, collaboration platforms, CRM systems, and project tools, then applies semantic search to retrieve relevant content across sources.

### Faster work with fewer interruptions
**Benefit copy**  
Less time spent searching and fewer repeat questions free teams to focus on meaningful work and speed up onboarding.

**How it works**  
Employees ask in natural language and get concise, cited answers. Built-in analytics highlight unanswered questions and knowledge gaps so admins can improve content over time.

## How It Works

### Connect your systems
Link Atlas Knowledge to your document repositories, collaboration platforms, CRM, and project tools. Existing user permissions remain in place.

### Ask in natural language
Employees ask questions the way they speak. Semantic search retrieves relevant content, and the system composes an answer grounded in approved information.

### Verify and improve
Each answer includes citations with links to the original sources. Admins use analytics on search activity and unanswered questions to strengthen content.

## Enterprise Trust and Governance

### Headline
Confidence from approved sources and existing permissions

### Copy
- Approved company content: Answers are grounded in your organization’s approved materials, not public data.  
- Citations and source verification: Every response includes citations with links back to the original content so employees can validate the answer.  
- Existing permissions and access controls: Atlas Knowledge respects the permissions you already enforce, ensuring employees only see information they’re authorized to access.

## Why Atlas Knowledge

- Traditional enterprise search returns documents, not answers. Atlas Knowledge delivers concise responses with citations, reducing the need to sift through multiple files.  
- Public AI assistants lack access to internal systems and authoritative sources. Atlas Knowledge connects to your business tools and shows exactly where each answer comes from.  
- Manual knowledge discovery depends on coworkers and memory. Atlas Knowledge provides a consistent, reliable way to find approved information without interrupting others.
