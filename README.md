# AI Marketing Lab

Applied AI for Product Marketing

## Why This Exists

After leading product marketing for enterprise software and AI products, I became increasingly interested in a different challenge: building the systems that support great go-to-market execution.

This repository brings together practical AI applications for product marketing, including customer insight generation, competitive intelligence, messaging development, launch planning, and sales enablement.

The focus is not on demonstrating AI for its own sake. It is on solving real business problems with tools that marketing teams can put to work.

## Current Project

### AI Headline Generator

A tool that turns a product brief into headline options for websites, ads, email campaigns, and social posts.

## Design Principles

Each project is designed to:

- Solve a real product marketing problem
- Produce structured, reusable outputs
- Fit into existing go-to-market workflows
- Support human judgment rather than replace it
