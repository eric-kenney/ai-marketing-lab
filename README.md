# AI Marketing Lab

Applied AI systems for product marketing and go-to-market teams.

## Why This Exists

After leading product marketing for enterprise software and AI products, I became increasingly interested in building the systems that support great go-to-market execution.

AI Marketing Lab brings together practical applications for customer insight, competitive intelligence, positioning, launch planning, sales enablement, and marketing operations.

The focus is not on demonstrating AI for its own sake. It is on solving real business problems with systems that marketing teams can put to work.

## Featured Project

### AI GTM Launch Orchestrator

A launch operating system that transforms a structured product brief into a coordinated set of go-to-market deliverables.

The system was inspired by a production workflow I designed for an enterprise product marketing organization. The original workflow supports up to 37 launch deliverables and reduced launch-content production time by approximately 95%.

The public version will demonstrate the core orchestration model through five deliverables:

1. Messaging Source Document
2. Ideal Customer Profile
3. Data Sheet
4. Pitch Deck
5. Blog Post

All public materials are independently recreated using generic workflows, fictional products, and nonconfidential examples.

## Additional Systems

### Competitive Intelligence Agent

A system that monitors public competitor activity, identifies meaningful changes, and publishes a recurring competitive dashboard.

### SaaS ROI Calculator

A business-value tool that translates operational improvements into projected financial impact.

## Design Principles

Each system is designed to:

- Solve a real product marketing problem
- Begin with structured strategic inputs
- Produce consistent, reusable outputs
- Fit into existing go-to-market workflows
- Automate execution while preserving human judgment
- Include human review before publication
