# Architecture

## Overview

The AI GTM Launch Orchestrator is an AI-assisted workflow that transforms a structured product brief into a coordinated set of go-to-market deliverables.

Rather than generating each document independently, the system establishes a single source of truth for product messaging. Every downstream asset is derived from that shared foundation, improving consistency while reducing repetitive work.

The system is designed to accelerate execution—not replace product marketing judgment. Human review remains a required step before any deliverable is published.

---

## Objectives

The orchestrator is designed to:

- Standardize launch planning
- Reduce repetitive content creation
- Keep messaging consistent across every asset
- Capture product marketing best practices
- Produce reusable launch documentation
- Preserve human oversight

---

## Users

Primary users include:

- Product Marketing Managers
- Go-to-Market Managers
- Product Managers
- Marketing teams supporting launches

---

## Inputs

The workflow begins with a structured intake that captures information including:

- Product overview
- Customer problem
- Target audience
- Differentiators
- Pricing and packaging
- Competitive alternatives
- Proof points
- Launch details

---

## Outputs

The public implementation produces:

1. Messaging Source Document
2. Ideal Customer Profile
3. Product Data Sheet
4. Sales Pitch Deck
5. Launch Blog Post

Each output builds upon the Messaging Source Document instead of regenerating strategy from scratch. The private implementation produces up to 37 deliverables, based on the launch.

---

## Human Review

AI generates draft content.

Humans remain responsible for:

- Strategic decisions
- Accuracy
- Brand voice
- Legal review
- Final approval

The objective is faster execution, not autonomous marketing.
