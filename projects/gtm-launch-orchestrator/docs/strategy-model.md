# Strategy Model

## Purpose

The Strategy Model transforms product facts into marketing strategy. It serves as the canonical source of truth for positioning, messaging, and go-to-market decisions, ensuring every launch asset is built from the same strategic foundation rather than developed independently.

## Strategic Decisions

### Market Category

The product is positioned as an **Enterprise AI Knowledge Platform**.

While the product incorporates enterprise search and generative AI capabilities, it is positioned around the business outcome of helping employees quickly find trusted information rather than the underlying technology.

### Ideal Customer Profile

The ideal customer is a mid-market or enterprise organization (500–10,000 employees) with distributed teams, significant internal documentation, and multiple business systems that employees rely on to do their jobs.

These organizations have invested in collaboration and knowledge management tools but continue to struggle with information discoverability, inconsistent documentation, and repetitive internal questions.

### Competitive Landscape

The primary alternatives customers evaluate include:

- Enterprise search platforms
- AI assistants built into workplace productivity suites
- Knowledge management platforms
- Traditional document repositories and intranet solutions
- Existing internal processes for finding and sharing information

The launch messaging should acknowledge that most organizations are not replacing a single competitor. Instead, they are replacing fragmented knowledge experiences spread across multiple systems.

### Positioning

Atlas Knowledge is an enterprise AI knowledge platform that delivers trusted, cited answers from across an organization's business systems, helping employees find information in seconds instead of searching across disconnected tools. By making institutional knowledge instantly accessible and trustworthy, organizations can improve employee productivity, accelerate onboarding, and reduce the operational cost of finding information.

### Messaging Pillars

#### 1. Trusted Answers

Employees shouldn't have to wonder whether the information they find is accurate or up to date. Atlas Knowledge grounds every response in approved company content and provides citations to the original source, giving employees confidence in the answers they receive.

#### 2. Unified Knowledge

Employees shouldn't need to know where information is stored before they can find it. Atlas Knowledge connects to the systems organizations already use, creating a single, intelligent access point for trusted knowledge without requiring teams to change how they work.

#### 3. Faster Work

Every minute employees spend searching for information is time they aren't creating value. Atlas Knowledge helps employees find trusted answers in seconds, reducing interruptions, accelerating onboarding, and enabling teams to spend more time on meaningful work.

### Reasons to Believe

- **Grounded AI Responses:** Every answer includes citations to the original source content, enabling employees to verify information and build confidence in AI-generated responses.

- **Enterprise Security:** Atlas Knowledge respects existing user permissions and access controls, ensuring employees only receive answers from content they are authorized to access.

- **Integrated Everywhere:** Connects to existing enterprise systems—including document repositories, collaboration platforms, CRM applications, and project management tools—without requiring organizations to migrate or duplicate content.

- **Continuous Learning:** Built-in analytics identify unanswered questions, search patterns, and knowledge gaps, helping organizations continuously improve the quality and discoverability of their institutional knowledge.

### Differentiation

- **Trusted by Design:** Unlike AI assistants that generate answers without clear attribution, Atlas Knowledge provides cited, source-backed responses grounded in approved enterprise content.

- **Works Where Knowledge Already Lives:** Rather than replacing existing tools or requiring organizations to migrate content, Atlas Knowledge connects to the systems employees already use, making enterprise knowledge instantly accessible through a single interface.

- **Built for Enterprise Governance:** Atlas Knowledge combines AI-powered search with enterprise-grade security, permissions, and administrative controls, giving organizations the confidence to deploy AI without compromising governance.

### Message Guardrails

- **Don't lead with AI technology.** Focus messaging on business outcomes and customer problems before explaining how the technology works.

- **Avoid unsupported superlatives.** Don't claim to be the "best," "most intelligent," or "most accurate" unless those claims can be backed by evidence.

- **Never imply AI replaces employee judgment.** Position Atlas Knowledge as a tool that helps employees make better, faster decisions—not one that replaces human expertise.

- **Keep messaging grounded in real customer workflows.** Describe how the product solves everyday business problems instead of relying on abstract AI concepts or technical jargon.

## How This Is Used

The Strategy Model serves as the canonical marketing strategy for a launch. Every downstream artifact—including positioning documents, messaging frameworks, webpages, sales enablement, campaign briefs, analyst materials, and launch content—should align with these strategic decisions unless intentionally overridden.
