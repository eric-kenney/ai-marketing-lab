# Strategy Model

The Strategy Model is the canonical representation of product messaging inside the AI GTM Launch Orchestrator.

It is generated from the structured intake and serves as the source of truth for every downstream deliverable.

Unlike the Messaging Source Document, which is designed for humans to read, the Strategy Model is optimized for software.
