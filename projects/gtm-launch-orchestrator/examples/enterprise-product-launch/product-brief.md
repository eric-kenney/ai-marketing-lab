## Executive Summary

Atlas Knowledge is an AI-powered enterprise knowledge platform that helps employees quickly find accurate answers across company documentation, collaboration tools, and business applications.

The platform combines enterprise search with generative AI to deliver concise, cited responses grounded in an organization's approved content. Instead of searching through multiple systems or relying on outdated documentation, employees can ask questions in natural language and receive trustworthy answers with links to the original source.

Atlas Knowledge is designed for mid-market and enterprise organizations that want to improve employee productivity, reduce repetitive internal questions, and make company knowledge easier to discover and use.

## Customer Problem

Modern enterprises generate knowledge across dozens of systems, including documentation platforms, collaboration tools, CRM applications, project management software, and support systems. Employees often know the information exists but spend significant time determining where to look and whether the content they find is current.

Traditional enterprise search returns documents rather than answers, requiring employees to review multiple sources before finding the information they need. Public AI assistants can summarize information but lack access to internal content and often fail to identify authoritative sources.

As a result, employees lose productivity searching for information, repeat questions that have already been answered, and make decisions based on incomplete or outdated knowledge.

## Product Overview

### What It Is

Atlas Knowledge is an enterprise AI knowledge platform that connects to an organization's existing business systems—including document repositories, collaboration platforms, CRM applications, and project management tools—to help employees find trusted information through natural language search.

The platform uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to generate responses grounded in approved company content while respecting existing user permissions and access controls.

### Key Capabilities

- Connects to enterprise knowledge sources, including document repositories, collaboration platforms, CRM systems, and project management tools.
- Uses AI-powered semantic search to understand natural language questions and retrieve relevant content.
- Generates cited answers grounded in approved company information using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
- Enforces existing user permissions so employees only see information they are authorized to access.
- Provides analytics on search activity, unanswered questions, and knowledge gaps to help organizations improve their content.

### Out of Scope for This Release

The initial release does not include:

- Content creation or document authoring capabilities.
- Workflow automation or task execution based on AI responses.
- External customer-facing knowledge experiences.
- Custom AI model training or fine-tuning.
- Industry-specific compliance packages beyond standard enterprise security controls.

## Success Metrics

### Business Objectives

- Reduce the time employees spend searching for internal information.
- Improve employee confidence in AI-generated answers through cited responses.
- Increase adoption of enterprise knowledge resources.
- Reduce repetitive internal support and knowledge requests.

### Product KPIs

- Monthly Active Users (MAU)
- Average search response time
- Percentage of responses with supporting citations
- Successful search rate
- Average time to answer

## Risks and Assumptions

### Risks

- AI-generated responses may occasionally cite incomplete or ambiguous source material, requiring ongoing content governance.
- Adoption depends on the quality and organization of existing enterprise knowledge.
- Organizations with fragmented or outdated documentation may realize value more slowly.

### Assumptions

- Customers have centralized knowledge repositories that can be connected through supported integrations.
- Employees prefer natural language search over navigating multiple applications.
- Organizations will require human oversight for AI-generated responses in sensitive or regulated use cases.
