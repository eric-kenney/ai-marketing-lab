# Launch Plan

**Product:** Atlas Knowledge  
**Launch:** Version 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Launch Tier:** Tier 1  
**Target Launch:** Q4 FY2026  
**Owner:** Product Marketing

## Launch Overview

Atlas Knowledge is the first generally available release of the company's enterprise AI knowledge platform. This launch introduces a secure, AI-powered experience that enables employees to find trusted answers across enterprise systems through natural language search. The launch focuses on establishing product positioning, driving customer awareness, enabling the sales organization, and generating initial customer adoption.

## Launch Objectives

### Business Objectives

- Establish Atlas Knowledge as the company's enterprise AI knowledge platform.
- Generate qualified pipeline and early customer adoption.
- Build market awareness among IT and business decision-makers.

### Launch Objectives

- Equip the sales organization with consistent positioning and messaging.
- Publish core launch assets across marketing, sales, and web channels.
- Enable customers and prospects to understand the product's value and key use cases.

## Launch Scope

### In Scope

- General availability of Atlas Knowledge Version 1.0.
- Product positioning and messaging.
- Corporate website updates.
- Sales enablement materials.
- Product documentation.
- Customer announcement and launch campaign.
- Analyst and media briefings.

### Out of Scope

- Industry-specific solution packages.
- Additional language localization.
- Marketplace integrations not included in Version 1.0.
- Future AI capabilities planned beyond the initial release.

## Launch Timeline

| Phase | Objective |
| ------ | --------- |

## Dependencies

- Product development completes all launch-critical features.
- Product documentation is finalized before launch.
- Pricing and packaging receive executive approval.
- Legal and security reviews are completed.
- Sales enablement materials are approved before seller training.
- Marketing assets are completed before campaign launch.
- Customer Success training is completed before general availability.

## Risks

- Product readiness slips beyond the planned launch date.
- Messaging fails to clearly differentiate Atlas Knowledge from existing AI assistants.
- Sales teams are not fully enabled before launch.
- Customers have concerns about AI accuracy, security, or governance.
- Product adoption is slower than expected due to change management challenges.
- Competitive announcements reduce launch visibility.
